Meet the team
members

Digital Wealth Partners (DWP) is a pioneering Registered Investment Advisor (RIA) that specializes in digital assets and alternative investments, catering to Family Offices, High Net Worth Individuals (HNWI), and other RIAs. Leveraging a hedge fund type model and fee structure, DWP offers a unique blend of expertise in the burgeoning field of digital assets, including cryptocurrencies, blockchain-based assets, and other non-traditional investment opportunities. Our goal is to provide sophisticated, high-return investment strategies that are tailored to the risk tolerance and investment objectives of our discerning clientele.

Mission Statement

To redefine wealth management for the digital age by providing unparalleled expertise in digital assets and alternative investments, delivering customized, high-performance investment solutions to our clients.

Max Kahn
Chief Executive Officer

Max Kahn is the Chief Executive Officer of Digital Wealth Partners. With over a decade of experience in financial services and business strategy, Max specializes in guiding institutions and individuals through the evolving digital asset landscape.

Before joining DWP, Max served as Director of Strategy at Digital Asset Research and at YieldX, where he led institutional partnerships, product launches, and oversaw compliance processes. His earlier roles include Director of Futures Operations at National Securities Corporation, where he optimized investment platforms and enhanced risk management. Over the course of his career, Max has managed portfolios exceeding hundreds of millions of dollars across retail and institutional clients.

Max also contributes thought leadership on digital asset strategies and has been pivotal in launching groundbreaking indexes and financial solutions. He is a licensed securities professional.

Matthew Snider
Chief Investment Officer

Matthew Snider is the Chief Investment Officer of Digital Wealth Partners. He is a Registered Investment Advisor Representative, public speaker, guest lecturer, and published author of "Warren Buffet in a Web3 World".

Matthew began his post-MBA career as a management consultant at both Big4 and boutique firms specializing in financial / liquidity risk management and marketing data analytics. Beginning in 2016, he became an active participant in many nascent cryptocurrency projects and began engaging executive teams about the benefits of building go-to-market solutions for blockchain-based innovations.

He received his MBA from Loyola Marymount University and his BA in economics from Boston University.

Tom Teal
Head of Financial Planning

After graduating from Virginia Tech with a degree in Electrical Engineering, Tom spent his early career in technical product management, business development, and managing lines of business. While this provided an excellent opportunity to use his problem-solving skills and strategic focus but lacked personal impact. This led him to make a career change to financial services and to become a Certified Financial Planner (CFP®) to help individuals lead better financial lives.

An advantage to becoming a CFP later in his career is that Tom and his wife have sat in the client seat of other advisors. This provided a unique perspective that helps him empathize with client needs, challenges, and goals. It also led Tom to focus on helping people find financial balance. This "Enjoy today, plan for tomorrow" mindset requires a holistic approach and expertise in financial life planning. Tom's ability to communicate complex planning topics simply and clearly fosters open discussion and encourages client questions. Tom is always looking for new and innovative ways to positively impact clients' lives.

Tom is a happily married father of two. He enjoys spending time in the outdoors and trying to keep up with his wife at Crossfit. When he has time to relax, you can find him on the patio with a bourbon, cigar, and a good book.
