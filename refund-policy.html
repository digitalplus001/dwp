Onboarding Fee Refund Policy

Last updated: May 2026. This page is a placeholder — full policy coming soon.

Digital Wealth Partners charges an onboarding fee to cover the cost of account setup, compliance review, and initial advisory services associated with bringing a new client onto our platform. Please review this policy carefully before submitting your application.

Non-Refundable Onboarding Fee

Onboarding fees paid to Digital Wealth Partners are generally non-refundable once your account application has been received and the onboarding process has begun. This is because our team begins compliance review, KYC/AML procedures, and account setup work immediately upon receipt of your application and payment.

Exceptions

Refunds may be considered on a case-by-case basis in circumstances where Digital Wealth Partners is unable to onboard a client due to circumstances outside the client's control, or where a material error was made on our part. All refund requests must be submitted in writing via our contact page within 30 days of payment.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us at our contact page with your full name, application date, and the reason for your request. We will respond within 5 business days.
